# ANTHOLD Sound Direction v1

24 AI-generated review sounds, not the complete production library.

Start with the PDF and review-montage.mp3. The montage follows alphabetical WAV paths: six ambience excerpts, then disaster boulder, disaster warning, flight death, flight loop, knight death, knight hit, stronghold destroy, stronghold stage, UI confirm, UI powerup, then weapon ant, bee, beetle, butterfly, cricket, fly, scorpion, termite. One second of silence separates cues.

WAV imports have sample peaks limited to at most -3 dBFS; MP3 originals remain unprocessed generation output. No music audio: ElevenLabs music requires a paid plan. Prompts include music direction for later use.

Review samples are not sound-synchronised to actual gameplay. Six arena loops are candidates pending repeat listening. See audio-QA.csv for measurement checks and cue-catalogue.csv for design-only versus generated cues. No audio has been uploaded or wired into the teammate game.
