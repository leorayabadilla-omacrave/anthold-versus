# ANTHOLD arenas v3 — 6 backdrops with rules

Six duel arenas, each 1920x1088, drawn in the knight art style with full
backdrops (sky / garden / treeline / log interior / dunes / night porch).
The lane at the bottom is kept clear so knights and minions can stand on it.

These are playtest starting values — tune freely.

## The arenas

### 1. The Meadow (`arena-01-meadow.jpg`)
Open rolling dirt lane framed by grass and clover.
- **Rule:** no terrain effects — the fair, neutral duel. Tall grass at each
  end hides your knight from the other side's sight until they get close.
- **Tip:** best for learning a kit; ranged knights get full value from the
  long sightlines.
- **Ambient motion:** clouds drift, grass and flowers sway in the breeze.

### 2. The Garden Bed (`arena-02-garden-bed.jpg`)
A raised soil plateau in the middle with ramps on both sides.
- **Rule:** whoever holds the plateau gets +15% attack on knights below
  them. Walking up a ramp slows you by 20%.
- **Tip:** fight for the centre early; heavy knights (Beetle, Termite) love
  holding the high ground.
- **Ambient motion:** flowers and leaves sway, the watering can drips.

### 3. The Creek (`arena-03-creek.jpg`)
The lane dips into a shallow stream, crossed by stepping stones and a twig
bridge.
- **Rule:** walkers in the water move 30% slower; flyers (Butterfly, Fly,
  Bee) cross freely. The twig bridge holds one knight at a time.
- **Tip:** flyers dominate the middle; walkers should push minion waves
  across the bridge.
- **Ambient motion:** water flows around the stones, cattails sway.

### 4. The Log Hollow (`arena-04-log-hollow.jpg`)
Inside a fallen log, lit by glowing mushrooms and one beam of light from a
crack.
- **Rule:** the mossy stump in the middle is a raised platform anyone can
  hold. Standing in the light beam reveals you; outside it, knights further
  than half the lane away are shown only as outlines.
- **Tip:** assassins (Fly, Scorpion) love the dark edges; fight over the
  stump to keep vision.
- **Ambient motion:** mushrooms pulse with light, dust floats in the beam.

### 5. The Sandpit (`arena-05-sandpit.jpg`)
A hot sandy lane with a sunken pit in the middle.
- **Rule:** walkers in the pit move 25% slower and can't dash out. Every
  20s the sand shifts and pulls anyone in the pit toward its centre for 1s.
- **Tip:** knock rivals into the pit before an ultimate; flyers just hover
  over it.
- **Ambient motion:** heat haze shimmers, sand blows across the dunes.

### 6. The Night Porch (`arena-06-night-porch.jpg`)
Wooden porch boards at night, lit by a single lamp in the middle.
- **Rule:** the lit circle under the lamp gives +10% attack speed. Moths
  drawn to the lamp briefly block flyers' line of sight in the air lane.
- **Tip:** control the lamp circle; walkers can punish flyers while the
  moths swirl.
- **Ambient motion:** the lamp flickers, moths circle, stars twinkle.

## Notes
- All six share the same camera framing and lane height, so knights keep
  the same scale and foot position across arenas.
- Backdrops have more colour than the plain-cream knight sheets on purpose,
  so the fighters stand out.
- The ambient motions listed above are meant to be added as small looping
  effects on top of these stills (drifting clouds, flowing water, a
  flickering lamp, circling moths).
