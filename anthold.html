ANTHOLD

Storms, floods, boots and lawnmowers: the meadow is no safe place for a nest. Draw a shape and your ants build it out of their own bodies, from planks and curves to bridges, dams and rafts. Everything grows from the ground up, and falls if its support goes.

Survive each disaster to draft a skill, then pick your next route on the map. Damage to the nest carries over.
