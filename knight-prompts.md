# ANTHOLD knight prompts

Canva image prompts for the class knights in an action pose, each in its own home environment. Every knight follows one template: the Beetle Knight sheet.

## The template

- Layout: the knight full-body on the left in a dynamic action pose; the class crest large and still on the right, with the caption "<Class> colony crest" in bold serif and a short italic subtitle under it.
- Action: each knight mid-attack with its signature weapon, at a dramatic three-quarter angle, with a few motion streaks drawn in the same flat style. The whole knight stays in frame, never cropped.
- Style: flat ink-and-colour illustration on cream paper. Only black, the class colour and bone-white, with light hatching for shading.
- Environment: the class's home behind the knight, drawn in the same flat style in lighter tints of the palette so the knight stays the boldest thing on the page. It fades out before the crest, which sits on plain cream.
- Format: 16:9 landscape.
- Crest: the class sigil from the game, described exactly in each prompt. Always a split-field shield (left half black, right half the class colour) with the emblem counterchanged and outlined in bone-white. Each class section shows the sigil to match.
- Always attach beetle-knight-template.jpg (and ant-knight.png) as reference images, so Canva copies the layout and style.
- Flying classes (Butterfly, Fly, Bee) wear their wings as a cape, spread wide in the action pose. Ground classes have no cape.
- If a crest comes out garbled, generate the knight first and place the crest from the sigil set in Canva's editor.

Ground: no cape.

```
Character sheet in exactly the same template as the reference Beetle Knight sheet: cream paper background, flat ink-and-colour illustration using only black, ember red and bone-white with light hatching, 16:9. On the left, a full-body Ant Knight in Japanese tokusatsu hero style (sentai and rider-inspired) in a dynamic action pose at a dramatic three-quarter angle: lunging forward and thrusting a halberd whose head is two curved mandible blades, red rider scarf whipping back, hexagon-crest shield braced at the side, a few flat motion streaks behind the thrust. Helmet with elbowed antennae, angular compound-eye visor lenses, silver mandible faceguard; segmented pauldrons, narrow petiole belt with a round core, rounded gaster-shaped tasset. Behind the knight, the underground colony: tunnels and round brood chambers, roots hanging from the soil above, a column of marching ants, drawn in the same flat style in lighter tints of the palette and fading out before the crest panel. The whole knight stays in frame. On the right, a large still heraldic heater shield split down the middle (left half black, right half ember red); two curved mandibles rise from the bottom and curl inward toward a single round eye in the upper middle, counterchanged (red on the black half, black on the red half) with thin bone-white outlines and a bone-white dot in the eye; two short elbowed antennae stick out above the top edge, captioned "Ant colony crest" in bold serif with the italic subtitle "split field · mandibles counterchanged".
```
