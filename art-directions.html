ANTHOLD Versus · pick a look

Five art directions, one battle

Each panel draws the same moment: your nest on the left, an ant squad marching out, a living ant ladder reaching a butterfly, a rhino beetle advancing, and the enemy nest on the right. Tell me the letter you like, or mix them (say, B's world with D's bugs).

A · current

Field Journal

Ink and watercolor on paper, with crisp pixel bugs living inside the drawing.

  • Strength: distinctive; the world feels handmade and the bugs stay readable.
  • Watch for: two styles at once can look mismatched at small sizes.
B

16-bit Pixel World

Everything in pixels: dithered sky, layered pixel hills, pixel nests and bugs on one grid.

  • Strength: one consistent look, crisp on any screen, classic strategy-game feel.
  • Watch for: less unique; detail is limited by the grid.
C

Papercraft Diorama

Layered cut-paper scenery with soft shadows; bugs are little paper cutouts with white edges.

  • Strength: warm and tactile, reads like a tabletop war in miniature.
  • Watch for: soft shading needs care so units pop from the background.
D

Bold Toon

Thick outlines, flat bright colors and cel shadows; big-eyed, expressive bugs.

  • Strength: best readability in busy fights; each class gets a loud color identity.
  • Watch for: loses the naturalist charm of the current look.
E

Natural History Plate

Victorian engraving: sepia line-work, fine hatching, Latin labels, a plate border.

  • Strength: the most elegant and unusual; fits the field-notes theme.
  • Watch for: monochrome makes the two armies harder to tell apart, so it needs team color accents.
